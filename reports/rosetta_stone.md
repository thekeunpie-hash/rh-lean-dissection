### 1. Keyword Trap and Confirmation of Absence
Across the DuckDB index, `Stepanov` has **0 hits** across modules, declarations, and signatures, and `Borcherds` and `SO2` are likewise unrelated to $O(2,n)$ automorphic forms. Module-name matching on `O2` returns only **4 modules** case-sensitively (all dealing with abelian-scheme structure-sheaf cohomology `HO2` = $H^2(\mathcal{O}_X)$: `AbelianSchemeNoPrimitiveO2{,Statement}` and `SurjectivePullHO2OfPullHO1{,Statement}`), and only **5 modules** case-insensitively (adding `WeakShimuraTheory.FrobeniusFields.Two2`). The 3 `Wronskian` modules are likewise analytic Whittaker/Eisenstein differential-equation lemmas unrelated to Stepanov auxiliary polynomials.

### 2. Replacement Mechanism in the Actual Lean Code
The engine that high-multiplicity auxiliary polynomials over finite fields (Stepanov) and $O(2,n)$ automorphic determinants (multiplicity 2) were meant to provide—**"overwhelming the main term via high vanishing order/divisibility to force upper bounds on prime sums"**—is completely replaced in the actual codebase by the following 3-layer architecture:

1. **Replacement of Groups and Spaces — Unitary Groups $U(X_j), U(Y_j), U(W_j)$ over CM Fields Instead of $O(2,n)$**:  
   `Formalism.BasicNotions.Spaces` in [DataSpaces.lean:77-140](../lean/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataSpaces.lean#L77-L140) constructs a chain of Hermitian spaces (`HSpace E`) over a quadratic CM extension $E/F$ (`CMData F E`) of a totally real field $F$. The definite Hermitian spaces $X_j$ (rank $N(j) = k + j(t+1)$), the indefinite Hermitian space $Y_0$ (signature $(k+r_S, r_S+1)$, where $r=k+m-1$, $r_S=m/2-1$), and the spaces $W_j$ (rank $r$, signature $(k+j-1, m-j)$) form the arena for all automorphic maps.
2. **Arakelov/Bost Ensemble Slope Inequality Instead of Determinant Multiplicity**:  
   `Formalism.HN.SlopeIneq` in [Statement.lean:91-99](../lean/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/Statement.lean#L91-L99), `Formalism.HN.TheoremF` and `Formalism.HN.TheoremFSharp` in [StatementFrameF.lean:26-99](../lean/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/StatementFrameF.lean#L26-L99), and `Data.MeanBoundSecondForm` in [MeanBoundSecondForm.lean:151-190](../lean/src/Formalism/RankGapBounds/MeanBoundSecondForm.lean#L151-L190) perform an **ensemble-average comparison of Hermitian lattice slopes (log-covolumes)** for the evaluation maps (the Bessel period maps `D.div.Bk u`) rather than evaluating a single determinant.
3. **Inert Prime-Power Divisibility Amplification (`LinkG_at`) + $p$-adic Jets (`ThetaJet`) + Bideterminants (`bidetSpan`) Instead of Multiplicity Mimicry**:  
   - `Formalism.MainNotions.LinkG_at` in [GlobalTheoremInputs.lean:122-136](../lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalTheoremInputs.lean#L122-L136) forces the lattice-index gain `L.gain j w - L.tau w` at an inert prime place $w$ (`InertK D p`, i.e., `signal` or `inertClass`) to dominate the weighted von Mangoldt residue-class sum:
     $$\sum_{j \in \mathrm{lifts}(D)} \sum_{t \in T_w} \bigl(\mathrm{vonMangoldtClassSum}(j,t,p) - \mathrm{loG}(p)\bigr) - m\cdot \mathrm{flG}(p) \;\le\; \sum_{j \in \mathrm{lifts}(D)} \mathrm{gain}(j,w) - \tau(w).$$
   - Local divisibility-order computations are carried out by the $p$-adic formal power-series logarithm `Formalism.BasicNotions.jetLogG` ($\sum_{e \ge 0} \ell^{-e} X_{(\nu-2e,\beta)}^{\ell^{2e}}$) and `Formalism.BasicNotions.ThetaJet` in [DataJet.lean:13-64](../lean/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataJet.lean#L13-L64), together with the Fock-space bideterminant `Formalism.GradedWeylDivisibility.bidetSpan` in [StatementB.lean:15-69](../lean/src/Formalism/GradedWeylDivisibility/StatementB.lean#L15-L69) and the Gelfand–Tsetlin/Weyl dimension formula `weylGT` in [FinalT.lean:11-14](../lean/src/Formalism/ThetaLogCoordinates/ResidueClassEstimates/FinalT.lean#L11-L14).

## ② Weighted von Mangoldt Sum $\sum_{n \le X} \Lambda(n)(X/n - 1)_+ - X\log X + (1+\gamma)X$

- **Final Verdict**: **`[Same Concept, Renamed]`**

### 1. 1-to-1 Comparison with the Actual Lean Definitions
The first-order Riesz/Cesàro weighted von Mangoldt sum presented in the announcement is defined **literally identically—down to signs, constants, and the Euler–Mascheroni constant $\gamma$**—in [StatementOC.lean:11-21](../lean/src/Formalism/StatementOC.lean#L11-L21):

```lean
def Sz (x : ℝ) : ℝ := ∑ n ∈ Finset.Icc 1 ⌊x⌋₊, Λ n * (x / n - 1)
def Gz (x : ℝ) : ℝ := Sz x - x * Real.log x + (1 + Real.eulerMascheroniConstant) * x
def Schi {N : ℕ} (χ : DirichletCharacter ℂ N) (x : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  ∑ n ∈ Finset.Icc 1 ⌊x⌋₊, Λ n * (χ n).re * (x / n - 1)
def Gchi {N : ℕ} [NeZero N] (χ : DirichletCharacter ℂ N) (x : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  Schi χ x + (deriv (DirichletCharacter.LFunction χ) 1 / DirichletCharacter.LFunction χ 1).re * x
```

Since $x/n - 1 \ge 0$ for $1 \le n \le \lfloor x \rfloor$, `Sz x` is identically $\sum_{n \le x} \Lambda(n)(x/n - 1)_+$, and `weighted_von_mangoldt_sum_identity` in [WeightedVonMangoldtSumIdentity.lean:227-228](../lean/src/Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/HookFormulaTorsion/WeightedVonMangoldtSumIdentity.lean#L227-L228) proves the identity with the explicit truncated form for scale parameters $a, w > 0$:
$$w \sum_{n=1}^{\lfloor a/w \rfloor} \Lambda(n)\left(\frac{a/w}{n} - 1\right) \;=\; \sum_{n=1}^{M} \Lambda(n)\max\!\left(\frac{a}{n} - w,\, 0\right) \qquad (\lfloor a/w \rfloor \le M).$$

### 2. Extension to Cyclic Character Groups and Reduction to Multiplicative Windows
For a general Dirichlet character $\chi$, `Formalism.HC.An.G` and `Formalism.HC.An.gChi` in [Analytic.lean:14-24](../lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/Analytic.lean#L14-L24) together with `Formalism.HC.Level.Gm` in [ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean:32-38](../lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L32-L38) define the weighted sum over a cyclic character group $\Lambda$:
$$\Lambda.\mathrm{Gm}(y) \;=\; G_z(y) + \sum_{\psi \in \Lambda.\mathrm{Nt}} \Lambda.\mathrm{wt}(\psi)\,\operatorname{Re}\bigl(g_\psi(y)\bigr), \qquad \Lambda.\mathrm{wt}(\psi) \in \bigl\{1,\, (3\Lambda.o + 1)^{-1}\bigr\}.$$
Subsequently, the Pontryagin duality expansion `AskMainBalance` in [FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean:98-101](../lean/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L98-L101) ($\mathrm{ASK} - \mathrm{MAIN} = \mathrm{De}$, proved via `colour_balance` in [StronglyIrreducible.lean:695](../lean/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ChainSlopeWindows/StronglyIrreducible.lean#L695)), together with the Landau oscillation statement `Formalism.HC.Level.FormBAt` in [Window.lean:30-31](../lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/Window.lean#L30-L31) and `Formalism.HC.level_of_tiedWin` in [TopWin.lean:68-69](../lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/TopWin.lean#L68-L69), deduce `ZeroFreeAll` (absence of zeros in $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1/2$) from the upper bound $\Lambda.\mathrm{Gm}(x) \le x^{1/2 + (e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}$ over infinitely many multiplicative windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$.
